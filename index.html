    Tips for a complete recording
    +    
    
    Safari can't guarantee a complete recording. Test with a short recording before an important meeting.
    
    
      
      
    • 
        
      Keep MIA open on screenDon't press the lock button or switch apps while recording. MIA tries to keep the screen awake.
      • 
      
    • 
        
      Turn on Do Not DisturbAlso turn off Low Power Mode. If the screen still dims, set Auto-Lock to Never.
      • 
      
    • 
        
      A call can stop recordingThe audio so far is saved. When you come back, tap Resume to record the next part.
      • 
      
    • 
        
      Keep a backupiOS can clear browser storage. For an important meeting, tap Export → Save to Files as a backup copy.
      • 
      
    • ?
        
      Stop the mic questionOn iPhone open Settings → Apps → Safari → Microphone → Allow.
      • 
      
    • ?
        
      Online meetingPlace your iPhone near the laptop speaker. MIA records the room sound.
